## 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

A foundational principle of digital audio forensics is that a model cannot be considered reliable based solely on within-dataset cross-validation. Real-world criminal recordings originate from diverse neural vocoders, acoustic channels, and unknown compression pipelines.

In Phase 6.3, the trained forensic classifiers were evaluated against **300 independent, external audio exhibits** from the **Fake-or-Real (FoR)** forensic benchmark. The test cases include:
1. **Clean Synthetic Speech:** Speech generated by commercial text-to-speech (TTS) neural models (e.g., Amazon Polly, Google TTS, Microsoft Azure).
2. **Clean Genuine Speech:** Authentic human utterances from diverse international speakers.
3. **Acoustic Re-recorded Speech:** Recordings played through physical loudspeakers and re-captured in real room environments with ambient reverberation.

### Key Finding:
Cross-dataset generalization exhibits a substantial performance drop across all classical classifiers:
- **Clean TTS Speech:** Calibrated SVM achieves **51.50% accuracy** (Specificity: 62.00%, Recall: 41.00%), outperforming Random Forest (39.50%) and Logistic Regression (23.00%).
- **Acoustic Re-recording Channel:** Accuracy deteriorates to **38.00%** with synthetic recall falling to **8.00%** ($TP = 4 / 50$). 

*Forensic Conclusion:* Room impulse reverberation, loudspeaker transfer functions, and microphone colorations severely mask classical spectral vocoder artifacts. Classical MFCC features trained on direct-line voice conversion do **not** reliably generalize to acoustic re-recordings or unseen neural TTS engines without channel compensation.

## 4. ROOT CAUSE ANALYSIS OF PERFORMANCE DECAY

1. **Acoustic Masking via Re-Recording:**
   - When synthetic speech is played through physical speakers into a physical room, the room's reverberation (early reflections and late diffuse tail) acts as an acoustic lowpass/comb filter.
   - This physical filtering blurs the sharp spectral discontinuity artifacts in higher MFCC coefficients ($c_{10} - c_{20}$) that the SVM relies upon to detect vocoder phase inconsistencies.
   - Consequently, the model classifies re-recorded fake audio as genuine human speech ($FN = 46 / 50$).

2. **Synthesis Method Discrepancy:**
   - Training Data: **Voice Conversion (VC)** via spectral mapping (pitch contour shifting, timbre morphing).
   - FoR Test Data: **End-to-End Neural Text-to-Speech (TTS)** using modern HiFi-GAN / WaveGlow / MelGAN vocoders.
   - Modern neural vocoders produce natural pitch contours and harmonic structures that closely mirror human vocal cords, eluding 26-dimensional spectral statistics trained only on older voice conversion algorithms.

3. **Linear Model Failure:**
   - Logistic Regression completely collapsed on external data (Recall: $1.00\%$ and $0.00\%$), proving that linear decision hyperplanes cannot capture cross-domain vocoder distributions.

## 5. STATUTORY & EVIDENTIARY IMPLICATIONS FOR EXPERT WITNESS TESTIMONY

Under Federal Rule of Evidence 702 and Daubert/Frye admissibility standards:
1. **Known Error Rates:** The known False Negative Rate (FNR) on external acoustic re-recordings is **$92.00\%$** ($46/50$). An examiner testifying in court must state that acoustic re-recording renders this detection pipeline ineffective.
2. **Channel Authenticity Requirement:** Automated analysis should only be attempted on direct digital extractions (bitstream extractions from devices), never on re-recorded microphone eavesdropping or room surveillance audio.
3. **Mandatory Out-of-Distribution Warning:** If an exhibit's spectral profile indicates acoustic room coloration, the examination status must be downgraded to `INDETERMINATE (RE_RECORDED_CHANNEL)`.
