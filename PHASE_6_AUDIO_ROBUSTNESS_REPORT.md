## 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Digital voice recordings in criminal casework are rarely pristine studio exhibits. They frequently arrive degraded by mobile network telephony codecs (AMR, GSM, G.711), lossy WhatsApp/Telegram voice note compression, background environmental acoustic noise, or room reverberation.

Phase 6.5 executes controlled acoustic perturbation testing across 9 realistic forensic degradation axes to measure:
1. **Prediction Stability:** Does the model maintain its classification when the exhibit is subjected to real-world channels?
2. **Recall/Sensitivity Decay:** At what noise or bandwidth threshold does synthetic vocoder detection fail?
3. **Forensic Vulnerability Points:** Which acoustic channels completely blind the detector?

### Major Scientific Finding:
1. **The Telephony Blind Spot:** Under standard G.711 telephony filtering (300 Hz – 3,400 Hz), **synthetic detection recall completely collapses to 0.00%** ($TP = 0 / 10$). Because high-frequency spectral cues above $3.4$ kHz are filtered out, 100% of synthetic voice conversions are misclassified as genuine human speech.
2. **Noise Masking Threshold:** Additive noise at $\text{SNR} = 30$ dB preserves performance ($\text{Recall} = 80.0\%$), but decreasing SNR to $20$ dB drops recall to $30.0\%$, and $10$ dB drops recall to $10.0\%$.
3. **Lossy Companding Resilience:** 8-bit $\mu$-law logarithmic companding preserves synthetic detection ($\text{Recall} = 90.0\%$, $F_1 = 0.7200$).

## 2. CONTROLLED STRESS-TESTING METHODOLOGY

Controlled acoustic transformations were applied to 20 balanced evaluation exhibits (10 genuine, 10 synthetic) from independent test recordings:

```
Acoustic Degradation Pipeline
├── Clean Baseline:               Unmodified standardized 16 kHz audio
├── Gaussian Additive Noise:      SNR = 30 dB (Mild studio noise)
│                                 SNR = 20 dB (Typical indoor office noise)
│                                 SNR = 10 dB (Heavy street / vehicular noise)
├── Telephony Bandpass:           G.711 standard: 300 Hz to 3,400 Hz (FFT smooth roll-off)
├── Lossy Codec Quantization:     ITU-T G.711 8-bit mu-law companding & expansion
├── Acoustic Reverberation:       Synthetic room impulse response (RT60 = 0.3s)
├── Digital Pre-Amp Clipping:     Hard saturation threshold at 0.70 of peak
└── Narrowband Resampling:        Downsampled to 8 kHz (cellular) & reconstructed to 16 kHz
```

## 4. SCIENTIFIC ROOT-CAUSE ANALYSIS

### 4.1 Why Telephony Filtering Eliminates Synthetic Vocoder Artifacts
Synthetic speech generated by neural vocoders and statistical voice converters exhibits phase inconsistencies, harmonic smearing, and high-frequency energy anomalies primarily located between **$3.5$ kHz and $8.0$ kHz**.
- In standard landline and legacy cellular networks (G.711 / AMR-NB), an analog or digital bandpass filter sharply attenuates all spectral content below $300$ Hz and above $3,400$ Hz.
- When this filter is applied, the high-frequency spectral bands corresponding to `mfcc10` through `mfcc20` lose their discriminative synthetic signature. The classifier defaults to predicting genuine speech, resulting in a **100% False Negative Rate**.

### 4.2 Additive Noise Masking
- As Gaussian noise power increases ($\text{SNR} \le 20$ dB), random energy elevates the spectral floor in the Mel bands, drowning out subtle vocoder discontinuities.
- At $\text{SNR} = 10$ dB, synthetic detection recall drops to $10.0\%$, confirming that noisy audio cannot be reliably assessed by spectral statistics alone.

## 5. FORENSIC PROTOCOLS & EVIDENTIARY SAFEGUARDS

To comply with digital forensic best practices (SWGDE Guidelines):
1. **Mandatory Quality Pre-Screening:** The quality screening module in `src/audio_quality.py` must reject audio exhibits with estimated $\text{SNR} < 15$ dB.
2. **Telephony Exclusion Protocol:** If an exhibit's effective bandwidth is identified as $\le 3,500$ Hz (telephone audio), automated classification must be suppressed and flagged as `INDETERMINATE (BANDWIDTH_RESTRICTED_TELEPHONY)`.
3. **Derivative Sample Preservation:** Representative degraded audio files generated during stress testing are persisted in `results/robustness/derivative_samples/` for auditability and laboratory inspection.
